Room shows one room's readings, two to four of them, with the room name on top and a segmented ring around them; it is a page in the carousel you turn through.

Consumer provides: the room name (12 characters or fewer) and, for each sensor the room has, the reading and its state. Readings always come in the order Temperature, Humidity, CO2, PM2.5; a room without a sensor for a metric just has fewer readings.

- Layout by count, nothing else on screen: **2 readings** side by side (centres x 70 and 170, `value-l`, label at top 89); **3 readings** one centred on top (x 120, top 60) and two below (x 78 and 162, top 126), `value-m`; **4 readings** two rows of two (x 78 and 162, tops 60 and 126), `value-m`. Each reading is its metric and unit in `label` / `ink-muted` over the value.
- **Segmented ring:** the ArcGauge ring (232 px, rotation 135, 270°, `ring-w` 10, rounded) split into one segment per reading, in reading order clockwise from the bottom left, `ring-gap` (12°) apart; segments share the 270° equally (2 readings: 129° each, 3: 82°, 4: 58°; integer degrees, the last one ends at 270°). Each segment is a small ArcGauge of its reading: `track` background, indicator from the segment's start to the reading's place in its ring range (the same range as its Sensor page), in the state colour (`ok`, `warn`, `danger`); offline, an empty indicator on the track. On arrival every segment sweeps from 0 to its value in 250 ms, like the Sensor ring; later changes ease in 250 ms. With one reading the ring is the whole 270°.
- Room name in `label` / `ink` at top 30, with its optional RoomIcon (16 px, 6 px gap) in front, icon and name centred as one line; PageDots at top 222. No separator lines, no status words. Keep everything inside the content circle (r 98).
- State is the colour of the value alone: `ink` when OK, `warn` for WARN, `danger` for ALERT. An offline sensor shows `--` in `ink-muted` and keeps its place. The state spelled out in words is one tap away, on the Sensor page.
- Touch: each reading is a target (its half or third of the content circle, never smaller than 82 x 58 px); tapping opens that metric's Sensor page. Pressed state: `surface-2` fill, `radius-md`. Encoder press or swipe up opens the room's Sensor pages from Temperature.
- Swipe left and right (or turn) moves to the next page of the carousel (the next room, then Weather, then Electricity), without wrapping; long press, swipe down or a 600 ms touch hold opens the Settings list from here (top level).
- Don't label the ring segments, show a second room, add icons or trends, or shrink the type to fit a fifth reading.
